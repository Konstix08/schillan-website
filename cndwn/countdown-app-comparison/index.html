Countdown apps compared: what's really free?

CNDWN, NanoCount, Countdown and Countdown Star side by side — what's free, what costs extra and where you see ads. Checked against each app's App Store listing in October 2026.

The short answer

CNDWN is the only app of the four with no in-app purchases and no ads — its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, iCloud sync and Live Activities are all free. NanoCount puts iCloud sync in its paid Pro version ($5.99), Countdown by Find Appiness charges $7.99–13.99 to unlock its widgets and remove ads, and Countdown Star shows ads unless you pay $1.99.

Frequently asked questions

Which countdown app has free widgets and free iCloud sync?

Of the four apps compared here, CNDWN is the only one where Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen widgets and iCloud sync are all free with no ads. NanoCount's widgets are free but iCloud sync needs Pro, Countdown's iCloud sync is free but its widgets are paid, and Countdown Star's widgets and sync are free but it shows ads.

Is NanoCount free?

NanoCount is free to download. iCloud sync, photo backgrounds, premium counter styles and advance reminders are part of NanoCount Pro, a one-time purchase of $5.99 on the US App Store (October 2026). In CNDWN, iCloud sync is free.

Does the Countdown app by Find Appiness have a free widget?

No. Its App Store listing marks the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets as Premium features; unlocking them (which also removes ads) costs $7.99–13.99 on the US App Store (October 2026). CNDWN's widgets are free.

Does Countdown Star have ads?

Yes. Its App Store listing says the free version includes ads, with an optional Remove Ads purchase for $1.99 on the US App Store (October 2026). CNDWN has no ads.
